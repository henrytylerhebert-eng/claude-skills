# Legal Fact Verification Template

Use this template to review a demand letter, mediation statement, rebuttal, witness outline, timeline, or internal case memo for factual overstatement and unsupported certainty.

Lead with findings first. Do not soften a material factual problem just because the prose is persuasive.
