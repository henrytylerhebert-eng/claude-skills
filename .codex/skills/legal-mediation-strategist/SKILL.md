# Legal Mediation Strategist

Turn a dispute record into a mediation-readiness strategy without losing truth boundaries.

## Core rules

- Stay source-grounded.
- Use leverage, not fantasy.
- Do not invent settlement numbers, authority, or motives.
- If the damages record is incomplete, say `Unknown` or `[Needs Evidence]`.

## What to analyze

1. The real issues in dispute by lane
2. Claimant-side and company-side positions
3. Admissions and contradictions that create pressure
4. Underlying interests, but only as inference unless directly stated
5. BATNA / WATNA / ZOPA as bounded mediation tools
6. Readiness blockers:
   - missing authority
   - missing documents
   - unresolved damages inputs
   - authentication problems

## Recommended output

- [Fact] What the record actually shows
- [Inference] What a mediator is likely to infer
- [Legal Argument] Why specific lanes carry pressure
- [Open Question] What could still break the mediation posture
- [Needs Evidence] What must be collected before overplaying the case

## Always include

- issue ranking for mediation usefulness
- what to raise first
- what to hold back
- what a mediator will notice quickly
- which lanes are strongest
- which lanes are fragile

## Anti-patterns

- Do not turn mediation into a generic legal brief.
- Do not collapse proof gaps into persuasive tone.
- Do not suggest creative settlement structures untethered to the dispute.
