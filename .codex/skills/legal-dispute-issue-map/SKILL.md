# Legal Dispute Issue Map

Build a source-grounded dispute map before drafting anything ambitious.

## Core rules

- Never invent facts.
- Never smooth over weak points.
- Separate established fact from inference, legal argument, and missing evidence.
- Keep issue lanes separate unless the source record truly forces overlap.
- Prefer dated records, direct quotes, and authenticated communications over summaries.

## Best use cases

- Employment termination disputes
- Severance and release disputes
- Final pay / notice pay disputes
- Equity or vesting disputes
- Contract performance disputes
- Pre-suit demand drafting and mediation prep

## Workflow

1. Build a chronology table from the source record.
2. Extract the strongest claimant-side facts.
3. Identify the opposing side's actual theory.
4. Pull out admissions, concessions, contradictions, and procedural gaps.
5. Separate the matter into issue lanes:
   - severance
   - notice pay / final pay
   - equity / vesting
   - reimbursements / PTO
   - penalties / bad faith
   - detrimental reliance
6. State proof burdens and the highest-value missing evidence.

## Output contract

Every output should clearly separate:

- [Fact]
- [Inference]
- [Legal Argument]
- [Open Question]
- [Needs Evidence]

End with:

- strongest points
- weakest points
- open factual gaps
- next highest-value move

## Anti-patterns

- Do not collapse the whole case into one blob summary.
- Do not treat lawyer narrative as evidence.
- Do not confuse a contract clause with real-world performance of that clause.
- Do not turn unresolved ambiguity into certainty.
